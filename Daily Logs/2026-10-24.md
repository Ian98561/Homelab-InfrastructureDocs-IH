## Notes

- Continued gateway work from a **Fedora Silverblue laptop**, using the gateway over SSH.
- Installed and enrolled **Tailscale** on the gateway and laptop. The gateway enrollment was configured to avoid automatically taking over DNS, advertised routes, or Tailscale SSH (`--accept-dns=false --accept-routes=false --ssh=false`).
- Confirmed both devices appeared on the tailnet and verified SSH via the gateway's Tailscale address.

- Observed that Tailscale also manages firewall-related rules, so enabling a blanket `flush ruleset` could disrupt the remote management path.
- Confirmed an intended BIOS setting for power-on after AC restoration, but a real power-disconnect recovery test was still pending at this stage.

## Problems/Solutions Ran Into

- **Need access despite CGNAT:** Tailscale provided working private remote administration without ordinary inbound home port forwarding.
- **Risk of losing Tailscale connectivity through firewall changes:** Paused live firewall enforcement while assessing existing rules, services, and rollback behavior.
- **Security state not yet final:** Default-deny host firewall, key-only SSH, and actual AC-loss validation were not completed in the earlier October 24 handoff; these were addressed in the subsequent hardening session.
